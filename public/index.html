House Rules

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards count as 10. An ace counts as 1 or 11, whichever works best for your hand.

Hit to take a card, or stand to keep your total. The dealer draws to 16 and stands on every 17, including soft 17. A total of 21 ends your turn automatically.

An ace and a ten-value card on the opening deal is blackjack. If both hands have blackjack, it’s a push. This table uses one freshly shuffled 52-card deck per hand and plays with Hit and Stand.
